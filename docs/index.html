強度旋鈕：引導係數 wIntensity knob: guidance coefficient w

同一情緒、同一句文本，僅改變推論時的引導係數 w。w=0 為不使用引導的一般條件式生成；w 越大，情緒條件與空條件之間的差異方向被放大得越多。Same emotion, same sentence; only the guidance coefficient w changes at inference. w=0 is plain conditional generation; larger w extrapolates further along the direction between the emotion condition and the null condition.

聽什麼What to listen for由左至右聽音高、音量與語速的變化。恐懼與厭惡在 w=2 時語速明顯加快。Left to right: pitch, loudness and speaking rate. Fear and disgust speed up noticeably at w=2.

控制範圍的但書：資料範圍內與分佈外What the numbers hide: inside vs outside the data range

同一模型、同一情緒（憤怒）、同一句英文文本，僅改變所指定的激發度。第一格為訓練資料幾乎未出現的極端設定，其餘兩格落在訓練資料實際涵蓋的範圍內。Same model, same emotion (angry), same English sentence; only the requested arousal changes. The first cell is an extreme setting the training data barely contains; the other two lie inside the range the data actually covers.

聽什麼What to listen for第一格與其餘兩格的差異明顯，但那是模型在分佈外的外插行為；資料範圍內（第二、三格）的差異小得多，這正是客觀數值與聽感之間落差的來源。The first cell clearly differs from the others, but that is extrapolation outside the data. Within the covered range the difference is much smaller, which is exactly why the objective numbers look better than the perceived effect.

未微調模型上的表徵方向注入Activation steering on the un-fine-tuned model

不更新任何權重，於語言模型第 16 層的連續表徵加上一個激發度方向，強度為 α。此為分析章節所用的聽測刺激。No weights are updated: an arousal direction, scaled by α, is added to the continuous representation at layer 16 of the language model. These are the listening-test stimuli used in the analysis chapter.
